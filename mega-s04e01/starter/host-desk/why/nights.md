# Why the check-out day is not a night

**Decision:** a stay's nights are check-out minus check-in, in whole days. The check-out day is never counted.

**Why:** the guest leaves on the check-out day, and the room is cleaned and let again that same day. Counting it charges the guest for a night nobody stays, and pays the host for it. The bank pays only what was booked, so the batch would be refused.

**So:** a stay from the 3rd to the 6th is 3 nights, not 4. Do not add one.
