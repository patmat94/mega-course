# Why payouts go out after check-out

**Decision:** a host's payout for a stay goes out on the check-out day, never at check-in.

**Why:** a stay can be cut short or cancelled after check-in, and then the guest gets money back. Paid at check-in, the house would have paid out money it may have to take back from the host.

**So:** hosts wait until the stay is over. That is on purpose. Do not move the payout date to check-in.

**The batch:** the date is the bank's job, not the batch's. The batch pays every task it gets on the day it runs, and the bank sends each payout on its stay's check-out day. Do not hold a task back for its date.
